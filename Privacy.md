# Isabot Privacy Policy

Isabot processes Discord messages in designated channels in order to participate in conversations and provide AI-generated responses.

Relevant message content and recent conversation context may be sent to a third-party AI service solely to generate Isabell's responses. We do not use Discord messages to train AI models or for advertising.

Isabot may also process Discord user IDs and related Discord account information when required for moderation and bot functionality.

Any data stored by Isabot is used only to operate the bot. Users may request deletion of data associated with them by contacting the server administrators.

By interacting in channels where Isabot is active, users acknowledge that their messages may be processed as described above.
